02 / The approach

A little structure.
A lot less guesswork.

Our approach to support is straightforward: understand the issue, explain the options, and work toward a practical next step.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Start by listening.

What’s happening, who it affects, and what you’ve tried. Context comes before a fix.

02 / EXPLAIN

Keep it clear.

Plain language helps you understand the problem and make an informed decision about what comes next.

03 / MOVE FORWARD

Focus on the next step.

A useful resolution starts with a sensible action and a clear explanation of what to expect.

03 / Before you begin

A few useful answers.

What information should I have ready?

Your device and operating system, the application involved, the exact error message, and when the problem started. A screenshot can help; remove passwords and personal information first.

Should I share a password?

No. Keep passwords, recovery codes, and other account secrets private. Explain the access problem without including credentials.

How does gr3y.io fit into the company?

gr3y.io focuses on IT support. gr3ylabs is the software and experimentation branch, and gr3ycapital is the parent company with a focus on finance and financial applications.

Where can I explore your IT tools?

Visit Gr3y Tools on GitHub for one-line PowerShell utilities for field IT work. Review each tool’s instructions before running it.
