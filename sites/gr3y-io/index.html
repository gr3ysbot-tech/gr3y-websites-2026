A few useful answers.

What information should I have ready?

Your device and operating system, the application involved, the exact error message, and when the problem started. A screenshot can help; remove passwords and personal information first.

Should I share a password?

No. Keep passwords, recovery codes, and other account secrets private. Explain the access problem without including credentials.

How does gr3y.io fit into the company?

gr3y.io focuses on IT support. gr3ylabs is the software and experimentation branch, and gr3ycapital is the parent company with a focus on finance and financial applications.

Where can I explore your IT tools?
