Streamer Opt-Out

Last updated: August 24, 2026

If you are a streamer and you do not want your content clipped, email optout@gr3y.io. We will remove your existing clips and stop monitoring your channel within 24 hours. You do not need to explain why, and you do not need to prove ownership beyond showing you control the channel.

1. What we do

gr3ylabs operates automated systems that capture short excerpts from public livestreams on Twitch and Kick, reformat them for vertical short-form video, and publish them to our own YouTube channels. Every published clip names the streamer it came from and links to their channel.

2. How to opt out

Email optout@gr3y.io from an address associated with your channel, or message us from the channel itself, and include:

  • Your channel name and platform (Twitch or Kick)
  • Whether you want existing clips removed, future clipping stopped, or both

If you cannot email from a channel-associated address, any reasonable demonstration that you control the channel is enough — a post, a DM from the channel account, or a message in your stream chat directed to us.

3. What happens next

Within 24 hours of receiving a request we will:

  • Set every existing clip of your channel to private, removing it from public view
  • Remove your channel from our monitoring list, so no further clips are captured
  • Confirm by reply when both are done

Removal is permanent unless you later ask us to resume. We do not require a reason, we do not ask you to reconsider, and we do not treat a request as an invitation to negotiate.

4. Third parties acting for a streamer

Management, agencies, and rights holders acting on a streamer's behalf may use the same address. Please state the relationship and the channel involved.

5. Copyright claims

This page is a courtesy process, not a substitute for the formal one. If you would rather file a copyright claim directly with the platform, YouTube's process is at support.google.com/youtube/answer/2807622. Using it does not affect your ability to email us as well, and we will act on an email faster than a formal claim will resolve.

6. Contact

Opt-out requests: optout@gr3y.io
Anything else: hello@gr3y.io
